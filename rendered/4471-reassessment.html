Two independent fixes to investigate

Shared parser. It reads dates month first while these tenants write day first. For example, R-41200 describes a reminder for 7 April being sent on 4 July. Respect the tenant’s intended date order on the parser path.

Booking form hint. booking-v3 displays MM/DD/YYYY, but interprets dates using the tenant’s day-first locale. People following that hint get the wrong day and month. This affects form reports from tenant-01, tenant-02 and tenant-09. Align the hint with the form’s interpretation; a shared-parser fix alone does not resolve this mismatch. App and email reports from those same tenants still belong to the parser cause.

Track the causes separately: shared parser — 379 reports and booking-v3 hint mismatch — 33 reports. The engineering dispositions below come from the course update; no independent production-patch verification is claimed.
